# Voice-of-Customer report

Source: `sample_reviews_synthetic.csv`, 24 reviews.

## Summary

Customers like the timeline view and the comments feature. The main frustrations are slow and unreliable reporting, onboarding that leaves teams lost, seat pricing for small agencies and a weak mobile experience.

## Pains

### Slow, hard-to-trust reporting (verified in 3 of 24 reviews)

> Reports take forever to load once you have more than a few projects. (review #3)

> Dashboard is slow and sometimes shows outdated numbers, so I double check everything in a spreadsheet. (review #19)

> Exporting reports to PDF breaks the layout every single time. (review #10)

### Onboarding leaves teams lost (verified in 3 of 24 reviews)

> The onboarding checklist skipped half the things I actually needed. (review #1)

> Took two weeks before the team actually started using it. (review #11)

> they stopped after day three and I was still lost. (review #17)

### Seat pricing feels unfair for small agencies (verified in 2 of 24 reviews)

> Pricing jumps too fast when you add one more seat. (review #4)

> We almost left because of the seat pricing. (review #20)

### Weak mobile experience (verified in 2 of 24 reviews)

> No decent mobile app. (review #8)

> Mobile notifications arrive hours late, which defeats the purpose. (review #22)

### Hard to find old tasks (verified in 1 of 24 reviews)

> Great timeline, terrible search. I can never find an old task. (review #13)

### Slow support on weekends (verified in 1 of 24 reviews)

> Support is slow on weekends. (review #14)

## Desires

### Reusable templates (verified in 2 of 24 reviews)

> I wish there were ready-made templates for client onboarding. (review #6)

> Templates would save me hours. (review #15)

### Customizable client reporting (verified in 2 of 24 reviews)

> I would pay more if reporting could be customized for each client. (review #24)

> I can't justify it to my boss without better reporting. (review #12)

### Invoicing integration (verified in 1 of 24 reviews)

> Integration with our invoicing tool would be a dream. (review #16)

## Objections

### Competitors offer cheaper guest access (verified in 1 of 24 reviews)

> Another tool offers unlimited guests for free. (review #20)

### Hard to prove the value to management (verified in 1 of 24 reviews)

> I can't justify it to my boss without better reporting. (review #12)

## Customer phrases worth reusing

- "I can finally see who is overloaded without asking anyone." (review #2). Use in: headline or LinkedIn hook
- "Switched from spreadsheets and never looked back." (review #23). Use in: testimonial-style line on a landing page
- "it keeps client feedback in one place instead of scattered across email." (review #18). Use in: feature description
